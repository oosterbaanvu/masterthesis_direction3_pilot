
## Stress-test feedback on development filings
The current controls (schema checks, historical sign and magnitude checks, period-jump check) were tested by injecting one error into otherwise unchanged filings. Share of injected errors that were MISSED:
- an amount changed to a different, plausible-looking amount (changed_amount): missed 51/54
- a fact that should be reported is missing (drop_fact): missed 54/54
- one digit of an amount mistyped (one_digit): missed 54/54
- the current-period and prior-period values of an element swapped (period_swap): missed 54/54
- a correct value reported under a related but wrong element (retag): missed 54/54
- a value reported 1000 times too large (scale_x1000): missed 10/54
- a value reported with the wrong sign (sign_flip): missed 51/54
